# 0018. Dispose of the template's employment kind record

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-25

## Context

The template moved one commit past the pin this deployment carried, and the inherited
folder gains its decision 0028, which lets an experience entry name one employment kind
or a list of them and has the door refuse an unknown kind or an empty list. This
deployment asked for it, because its newest role is a part-time contract and the field
could hold only one of the two. A re-alignment writes a record of the child's own naming
every record the inherited folder gained, with what it bound here and what changed, and
this is that record.

## Evidence

The template's source, suites, and schema document came over byte for byte, and on the
final tree the docs audit, the selftest, the lint, the type check, the suite, and the
build passed, with codespell naming nothing. The template's new tests were watched
failing against eight mutants where they were written.

## Options considered

- **Write the role's kind as raw text the old field let through.** The door would now
  refuse it, and the text would never have matched the type. Refused.

## Decision

The template's change to the shared source, its suites, and the career schema document
are carried whole, as decision 0006 requires, and the ledger gains the template's row.

| Record | What it bound here |
| --- | --- |
| [the template's 0028, Let a role name more than one employment kind](../inherited/0028-let-a-role-name-more-than-one-employment-kind.md) | The model, the door's check, the kind reader, a badge per kind on the experience page and the home page, search over each kind, the two suites, the schema document's line, and the ledger's row. The graduate assistant role names contract and part-time with it. |

## Consequences

A role on this site says every kind it is, and a kind the template does not define fails
at the file that names it.
