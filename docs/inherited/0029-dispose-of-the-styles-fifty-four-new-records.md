# 0029. Dispose of the style's fifty-four new records

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-25

## Context

The style moved past the pin this project carried, from a4b27eb to 48c2f30, and the
family refuses ratcheting, so the wave lands complete or not at all. The template gained
fifty-four decision records, 0085 through 0138, and the style's rule on re-alignment asks
for a record of the child's own that names every one of them with what it bound in the
tree and what changed, or that it bound nothing and why.

The largest change is the shape of the law itself. The style split its guide and its
rulebook into documents a reader takes in whole. The hard rules, the delivery gate, the
rules of landing, and the upstream report left the guide for files of their own under
`docs/`, and the rulebook's core now names the rules for living documents, the rules for
records, and the prose law as documents beside it. A living document is now bounded at two
thousand words of prose, and this project's guide, at 5,294 words, could not have met the
bound in its old shape.

Other changes bind tooling. The lint stack moved to ESLint 10, and the workflow gained a
landing job that skips a gate a branch run already passed. A dependency audit joined the
advisory tier. The docs audit took over the seams rule and gained checks for invisible
characters, bare record citations, the state file's schema, and the install scripts a
manifest approves.

## Evidence

On the final tree the docs audit agreed with its conventions. It printed one advisory,
the ledger's review row, and named the spelling advisory as not run, because no codespell
is installed on this machine. The workflow installs it, and the branch run's findings are
answered before the landing. The audit's selftest reported every rule firing, and the
edition tool's selftest passed. The lint passed under ESLint 10 with no finding, and the
type check, the 142 tests, and the build passed.

The dependency audit, run at the desk as the guide now asks, named advisories in vite,
js-yaml, source-map-js, @vitest/mocker, and postcss-selector-parser. Every one predates this
change. A dependency update changes behavior and this change is shape, so they are left to
the change after it.

## Options considered

- Carry the new documents and keep the guide's hard rules where they stood. Refused,
  because the rules would then live twice, and the guide alone would stand past the bound
  on living documents.
- Carry the style's rule documents byte for byte, the client stack's bullets included.
  Refused, because the rules of the tree would then name an HTTP layer, a query cache, and
  mock handlers this site does not have, and the style asks a child to re-adapt from the
  diff whatever it adapted at adoption.
- Keep the lint stack at ESLint 9. Refused, because the lint configuration is style-owned
  law, and the upgrade passed here with no finding.

## Decision

Recopy the rulebook's core and its three new documents, the gate, the rules of landing,
the adoption, reporting, and editions documents, both audit scripts, and the edition tool
whole, and carry the inherited folder to 0138. Build the rules of the tree and the rules of
conduct from the style's documents, with this site's adaptations in the places they held
in the guide. The content door, the localStorage overrides, and the dossier's colors stand
where the client stack's HTTP, query-cache, and color bullets stood, and this site's six
rules of its own follow them. The suites substitute without mock handlers, and the ledger
guards the site. Rebuild the guide around its index and its commands. Re-adapt the
baseline, the lint configuration, the test configuration, the manifest, the README, and
the workflow from the template's diff, re-pin the four rulebook documents and both audit
scripts in CI, and move the pin. The pins' comment now says what they catch, a copy
changed between alignments, and no longer claims to see the style move. The upstream file
opens one entry, on a link in the rules of conduct whose text names the old document. Each
gained record is disposed of below.

## Consequences

The tree carries the family's current law in the style's current shape. The guide is
short enough to read whole before every change, and each hard rule lives in one document a
reader takes in whole. A landing now runs the gate once, on its branch, and the run on main
checks only that no landed branch is left behind. Every later re-alignment adds a record
like this one, as long as its list of gained records.
