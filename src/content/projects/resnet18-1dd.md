A fully serializable 1D implementation of ResNet18 for sequence and signal data, incorporating improvements from the paper Bag of Tricks for Image Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks along with personal optimizations. Key enhancements include a three-layer convolutional stem, ResNet-B inspired stride placement, a ResNet-D inspired average-pooling shortcut, and downsampling reduced from five occurrences to two. Hardswish and Mish activation implementations are included, and the codebase integrates directly into TensorFlow and Keras pipelines.
