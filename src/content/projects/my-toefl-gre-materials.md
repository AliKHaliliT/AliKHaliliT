A curated collection of templates, study guides, and tips for the TOEFL iBT and GRE General Test, created to help with effective preparation for both exams. It includes writing and speaking templates for every TOEFL task, a flexible template for both GRE analytical writing essays, and a comprehensive quantitative reasoning guide covering essential formulas, concepts, and problem-solving strategies drawn from personal test prep experience.
