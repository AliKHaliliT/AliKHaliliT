A TensorFlow implementation of DeepTCN, the probabilistic multivariate time series forecasting model based on temporal convolutional networks introduced by Chen et al. in Neurocomputing (2020). The model produces quantile forecasts and supports both parametric and non-parametric loss functions over multivariate series. This fork extends the upstream project by returning the model's training history, which is not implemented in the main repository.
