The admin panel of the VITA ecosystem, named for Rome's hall of records. It edits every ledger of the record in the browser with no server behind it, keeps drafts in localStorage, and publishes by producing the same seed files the site builds from, as single downloads, as a zip of the whole record, or as atomic commits pushed straight to the site repository through a per-file three-way merge.
