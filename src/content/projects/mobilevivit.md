An implementation of MobileViViT (Mobile Video Vision Transformers), an adaptation of the MobileViT architecture designed for higher-dimensional tasks such as video and action recognition. It ships in S, XS, and XXS variants and is built with Python, TensorFlow, and Keras from modular components that can be reused independently or combined to construct new architectures. Documented utility modules, including custom training loops and video data generators, are included to ease understanding and extension.
