A fully serializable 3D implementation of ResNet18 for volumetric and video data, incorporating improvements from the paper Bag of Tricks for Image Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks along with personal optimizations. Key enhancements include a three-layer convolutional stem, ResNet-B inspired stride placement, a ResNet-D inspired average-pooling shortcut, and a temporal dimension that is downsampled only twice in the stem block. Hardswish and Mish activation implementations are included, and the codebase integrates directly into TensorFlow and Keras pipelines.
