A fully serializable 2D implementation of ResNet18 for image tasks, incorporating improvements from the paper Bag of Tricks for Image Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks along with personal optimizations. Key enhancements include a three-layer convolutional stem, ResNet-B inspired stride placement, and a ResNet-D inspired average-pooling shortcut in the residual connection. Hardswish and Mish activation implementations are included, and the codebase integrates directly into TensorFlow and Keras pipelines.
