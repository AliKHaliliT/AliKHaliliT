The resume builder of the VITA ecosystem. It pulls the record straight from a public site repository with no token, composes resumes and CVs from it, and exports PDF, Word, and LaTeX through one shared layout contract, so a setting means the same thing on the screen and in every format. The PDF renders entirely in the browser, embedded fonts and all.
