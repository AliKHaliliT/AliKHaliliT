A modernized, human-only subset of the PASCAL-Part benchmark that isolates person instances and provides pixel-wise polygon annotations for six body parts: head, torso, upper arms, lower arms, upper legs, and lower legs. The original annotations were released in MATLAB (.mat) format, which modern deep learning frameworks do not easily consume, so this project parses, filters, and converts them into accessible modern formats. The result is published on Roboflow for instant export to any current framework, natively supporting instance segmentation while the polygons can also be collapsed into standard bounding boxes for object detection.
