A personal operating system for a life record. Portfolio, digital garden, travel atlas, library, and microblog live in one static React app styled as a field dossier, with a generative ambient layer and an instrument-grade motion system, and everything is content-seeded down to the palette, the site identity, and the wording of the footer sign-off. Two public sister apps complete the ecosystem, TABULARIUM to edit the record and EPITOMA to condense it into resumes, and the whole family ships as an MIT template. This site runs on it.
