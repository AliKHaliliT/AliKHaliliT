A personal operating system for a life record: portfolio, digital garden, travel atlas, library, and microblog in one static React app, styled as a field dossier with a generative ambient layer and an instrument-grade motion system. Everything is content-seeded and owner-editable through a built-in admin, down to the palette, the site identity, and the wording of the footer sign-off, and a self-contained resume builder turns the same data into PDF, LaTeX, and Word documents. This site runs on it.
