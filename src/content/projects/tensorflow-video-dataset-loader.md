A customizable video dataset generator for TensorFlow that handles efficient loading, preprocessing, and batching of video data for classification tasks. It consumes a simple class-per-folder directory layout and exposes controls for batch size, output dtype, resizing, normalization, shuffling, and remainder dropping. The loader is compatible with distributed TensorFlow training, making it suitable for both research and production pipelines.
