An index repository linking the four BResNet16D variants: BResNet16-1DD, BResNet16-2DD, BResNet16-3DD, and BResNet16-2Plus1DD. These architectures are inspired by ResNet but built entirely from bottleneck residual layers for lightweight performance on edge devices, which caps the practical depth at 16 layers and gives the family its name. The modifications build on the Bag of Tricks for Image Classification paper, with variant-specific improvements documented in each repository.
