An index repository linking the custom-improved ResNet18D variants: ResNet18-1DD, ResNet18-2DD, ResNet18-3DD, and ResNet18-2Plus1DD. The models build on techniques from the paper Bag of Tricks for Image Classification with Convolutional Neural Networks, with additional optimizations and personal refinements tailored to each variant's use case. The D in the names comes from the ResNet-D modification described in that paper.
