Iranian patent for an optical seed drill performance monitoring system designed to stay reliable under dust exposure. Filed July 15, 2020 (application 139950140003003583) and registered October 27, 2020 as Patent No. 102676 by the Iranian Intellectual Property Center; the patent is held by the University of Tabriz with a 20-year protection term. International classification: A01C 7/00, A01C 9/08, A01C 5/06.
